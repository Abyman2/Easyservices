# EasyService Financial Feasibility Analysis

**Model type:** Illustrative pilot planning model, not an audited forecast.  
**Currency:** Ethiopian birr (ETB).  
**Decision use:** Compare pilot economics and identify assumptions that must be validated.

## 1. Key assumptions

| Driver | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Completed transactions | 2,400 | 8,000 | 20,000 |
| Average transaction value | 3,000 | 3,200 | 3,500 |
| Commission rate | 8% | 8% | 8% |
| Active subscribing providers | 20 | 60 | 120 |
| Provider subscription / month | 1,000 | 1,200 | 1,500 |
| Sponsorship and other revenue | 60,000 | 120,000 | 240,000 |

## 2. Revenue projection

| Revenue stream | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Transaction commission | 576,000 | 2,048,000 | 5,600,000 |
| Provider subscriptions | 240,000 | 864,000 | 2,160,000 |
| Sponsorship and other | 60,000 | 120,000 | 240,000 |
| **Total revenue** | **876,000** | **3,032,000** | **8,000,000** |

Formula: transaction commission = completed transactions x average transaction value x commission rate.

## 3. Operating cost projection

| Cost category | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Engineering and product | 1,800,000 | 2,400,000 | 3,200,000 |
| Sales and provider onboarding | 600,000 | 900,000 | 1,300,000 |
| Cloud, tools, and support | 480,000 | 720,000 | 1,050,000 |
| Legal, compliance, and security | 300,000 | 360,000 | 500,000 |
| Marketing and research | 450,000 | 650,000 | 900,000 |
| **Total operating cost** | **3,630,000** | **5,030,000** | **6,950,000** |

## 4. Operating result

| Metric | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Total revenue | 876,000 | 3,032,000 | 8,000,000 |
| Total operating cost | (3,630,000) | (5,030,000) | (6,950,000) |
| **Operating result** | **(2,754,000)** | **(1,998,000)** | **1,050,000** |

On these conservative assumptions, annual operating profitability begins in Year 3. Monthly break-even depends on the ramp and could occur around month 18-20; this must be recalculated with real salaries, gateway fees, taxes, refunds, and acquisition costs.

## 5. Sensitivity analysis

| Scenario | Main change | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Downside | 30% fewer transactions and 2 percentage points lower commission | Break-even moves materially beyond Year 3 |
| Base | Assumptions above | Profitability begins in Year 3 |
| Upside | 30% more transactions and 30% more subscriptions | Earlier operating break-even, subject to support capacity |

The most sensitive variable is completed transaction volume, followed by commission rate and customer acquisition cost. Registrations without completed transactions do not support the model.

## 6. Pilot investment envelope

A pilot should be funded as a staged experiment. Release funds in gates tied to provider activation, completed transactions, reconciliation quality, and repeat usage. Do not spend on national scale before the Addis pilot validates supply density and support economics.

## 7. Missing data to validate

- Actual engineering and support compensation.
- Payment gateway, tax, refund, and settlement costs.
- Customer acquisition cost and conversion rate.
- Provider churn and willingness to pay.
- Average booking value by category.
- Legal, licensing, insurance, and data-protection obligations.
- Cloud cost under expected traffic and media storage.

## 8. Feasibility conclusion

The concept is **conditionally feasible as a focused Addis Ababa pilot**, not yet proven as a national commercial business. The financial case depends on completing transactions, not catalogue size. Technical hardening, regulated payment integration, and evidence from a real provider/customer pilot are required before committing to full-scale investment.
